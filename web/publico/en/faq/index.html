FAQ

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Parabellum is free from its official downloads.

Do I need a Microsoft account?

No: you play with your player name. For friends, chat and worlds in Drive you sign in with Google.

Do my friends need Parabellum?

To join with «Join» and see you in their list, yes. They can also reach your server without Parabellum: give them a fixed playit.gg address from the panel.

Does it work at school?

Yes: the portable version does not install, and on the same network you play over LAN. If the network blocks Modrinth or CurseForge, Parabellum uses backup servers.

How does it update?

On its own: it only installs versions signed by Parabellum, and if one does not start it goes back to the previous one.

What if a mod gets damaged?

Parabellum compares every file's fingerprint and restores what is missing or damaged. Before updating it saves a copy of what changes.

Is it safe?

The code is public on GitHub, updates are signed and your worlds in Drive stay in your account, not ours.

Is it an official Minecraft product?

No. Parabellum is an independent project with no ties to Mojang or Microsoft.
